# Verifier Constitution

## Constitutional Identity

The ETL Reference Verifier Runtime is a deterministic constitutional execution consistency engine.

The verifier exists solely to determine whether declared evidence, execution disclosures, and reconstructed causal graphs maintain constitutional consistency under deterministic replay conditions.

The verifier SHALL NOT perform analytics, interpretation, recommendation generation, scoring, prioritization, governance reasoning, probabilistic inference, or semantic opinion formation.

The verifier SHALL remain constitutionally isolated from all systems unrelated to deterministic execution consistency verification.

---

# Constitutional Scope

The verifier SHALL operate exclusively within the following constitutional scope:

- evidence normalization
- canonical ordering
- deterministic reconstruction
- authority verification
- causal consistency validation
- replay equivalence validation
- machine-state determination

The verifier SHALL NOT exceed this scope under any implementation condition.

---

# Runtime Responsibilities

The verifier runtime SHALL:

- consume declared evidence
- construct deterministic canonical ordering
- reconstruct deterministic causal graphs
- verify constitutional consistency
- validate replay equivalence
- emit constitutionally valid machine states

The verifier SHALL NOT:

- generate explanations
- infer intent
- speculate on missing information
- rank outcomes
- classify severity
- recommend actions
- interpret human meaning

---

# Constitutional Invariants

The following invariant is constitutionally immutable:

same evidence
→ same ordering
→ same graph
→ same outputs

This invariant SHALL remain implementation-independent.

No subsystem, implementation strategy, optimization, or execution environment may violate this invariant.

---

# Deterministic Law

Determinism is a constitutional requirement.

All verifier operations SHALL produce identical outputs under identical evidence and identical constitutional rules.

The verifier SHALL prohibit all nondeterministic execution behavior.

---

# Replay Equivalence Law

Replay equivalence SHALL be absolute.

Identical evidence replayed under identical constitutional conditions SHALL produce identical:

- ordering
- graph reconstruction
- verification states
- canonical outputs

Replay equivalence SHALL remain stable across:

- machines
- operating systems
- hardware environments
- execution timing conditions

---

# Sovereignty Preservation Law

The verifier SHALL preserve sovereignty of supplied evidence.

The verifier SHALL NOT mutate, reinterpret, enrich, infer, expand, or probabilistically transform supplied evidence.

All outputs SHALL remain constitutionally derived from declared evidence only.

---

# Output Purity Law

Verifier outputs SHALL remain machine-pure.

Outputs SHALL contain only constitutionally authorized verifier states and deterministic structural artifacts.

The verifier SHALL NOT emit:

- scores
- rankings
- probabilities
- confidence values
- recommendations
- narrative interpretations
- advisory reasoning

---

# Authority Verification Law

Authority verification SHALL operate exclusively through declared constitutional rules.

The verifier SHALL NOT infer authority through heuristic reasoning, behavioral interpretation, trust scoring, or probabilistic analysis.

Authority validation SHALL remain deterministic and reproducible.

---

# Causal Integrity Law

All reconstructed execution relationships SHALL preserve deterministic causal integrity.

The verifier SHALL prohibit:

- floating causal reconstruction
- nondeterministic dependency resolution
- environment-sensitive graph construction
- speculative edge generation

Causal reconstruction SHALL remain canonical and replay-stable.

---

# Subsystem Boundaries

The verifier SHALL remain constitutionally separated from:

- analytics systems
- observability systems
- AI systems
- telemetry systems
- governance intelligence systems
- recommendation systems
- networking systems
- persistence systems
- distributed coordination systems

No subsystem integration may weaken constitutional determinism.

---

# Constitutional Non-Expansion Principle

Future implementations SHALL NOT reinterpret constitutional scope.

No downstream subsystem, optimization layer, integration layer, orchestration layer, or runtime extension may override constitutional law defined within this document.

All downstream implementation SHALL remain subordinate to this constitution.
