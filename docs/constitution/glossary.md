# Canonical Terminology and Glossary

# Constitutional Terminology Authority

All terminology defined within this document is constitutionally authoritative.

All downstream implementation, architecture, orchestration, reconstruction, and verification systems SHALL use these definitions without reinterpretation.

Semantic drift is constitutionally prohibited.

---

# Evidence

Evidence is the declared immutable input material supplied to the verifier for deterministic constitutional verification.

Evidence SHALL:

- remain immutable
- remain replay-stable
- remain deterministic
- remain free from inferred augmentation

Evidence SHALL NOT include speculative interpretation.

---

# Execution

Execution is the declared sequence of constitutionally relevant operations subject to deterministic reconstruction and verification.

Execution SHALL remain replay-equivalent under identical evidence and constitutional rules.

---

# Authority

Authority is the explicitly declared constitutional permission relationship recognized by deterministic verification rules.

Authority SHALL NOT derive from:

- inference
- trust scoring
- probabilistic reasoning
- behavioral speculation

Authority validation SHALL remain deterministic.

---

# Disclosure

Disclosure is the explicit declaration of constitutionally relevant execution information required for deterministic verification.

Undeclared information SHALL NOT be inferred.

---

# Projection

Projection is the deterministic representation of constitutionally derived execution structure constructed from declared evidence.

Projection SHALL remain replay-stable and implementation-independent.

---

# Reconstruction

Reconstruction is the deterministic rebuilding of constitutional execution structure from declared evidence and canonical ordering rules.

Reconstruction SHALL prohibit speculative structure generation.

---

# Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)

A DAG is the canonical deterministic causal structure representing constitutionally reconstructed execution relationships.

DAG construction SHALL remain:

- deterministic
- replay-stable
- causally ordered
- environment-independent

---

# Causality

Causality is the constitutionally valid deterministic dependency relationship between reconstructed execution events.

Causality SHALL prohibit:

- speculative dependency inference
- nondeterministic edge generation
- timing-derived ambiguity

---

# Verifier State

Verifier state is the constitutionally authorized machine output emitted by deterministic verification execution.

Verifier states are restricted exclusively to:

- VALID
- INVALID
- UNDECLARED
- INCONSISTENT
- UNKNOWN

No additional semantic interpretation is permitted.

---

# Canonical Ordering

Canonical ordering is the deterministic stable sequencing of evidence and reconstructed execution relationships.

Canonical ordering SHALL remain identical under identical replay conditions.

---

# Deterministic Replay

Deterministic replay is the constitutional guarantee that identical evidence under identical constitutional rules SHALL produce identical:

- ordering
- reconstruction
- traversal behavior
- graph topology
- verifier states
- outputs

Replay divergence is constitutionally prohibited.

---

# Constitutional Consistency

Constitutional consistency is the preservation of all deterministic constitutional invariants during verification execution and replay reconstruction.

Constitutional consistency SHALL remain implementation-independent.

---

# Immutable Identity

Immutable identity is the deterministic canonical identifier assigned to constitutionally relevant structures without mutation after derivation.

Identity mutation is constitutionally prohibited.

---

# Replay Equivalence

Replay equivalence is the constitutional guarantee that verification behavior remains invariant across replay conditions.

Replay equivalence SHALL remain:

- deterministic
- environment-independent
- replay-stable
- implementation-neutral

---

# Sovereignty Preservation

Sovereignty preservation is the constitutional requirement that supplied evidence remains free from mutation, reinterpretation, enrichment, probabilistic transformation, or speculative expansion.

The verifier SHALL preserve evidence sovereignty absolutely.

---

# Output Purity

Output purity is the constitutional requirement that verifier outputs remain machine-pure, deterministic, replay-stable, and free from interpretive augmentation.

Output purity prohibits:

- scores
- rankings
- confidence values
- explanations
- recommendations
- narrative interpretation

---

# Environment Independence

Environment independence is the constitutional requirement that verifier behavior remains invariant across hardware, operating systems, timing conditions, and execution environments.

Environmental variation SHALL NOT influence verifier outputs.

---

# Constitutional Determinism

Constitutional determinism is the immutable requirement that all verifier behavior remain reproducible, replay-stable, causally ordered, and free from nondeterministic execution behavior.

Determinism is constitutional law, not implementation preference.
